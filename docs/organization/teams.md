# Teams & Communication

This document outlines the high-level structure of our engineering organization and how we communicate.

## Engineering Domains

Engineering is structured around project, product, or domain boundaries. Specific team structures are defined by engineering leadership and repository owners.

## Communication

- **Team Chat**: For synchronous, ephemeral communication. Do not use team chat for critical technical decisions.
- **GitHub Issues/PRs**: For asynchronous, code-level discussions.
- **ADRs**: For recording architectural decisions (see [ADRs](../architecture/adr/README.md)).

Refer to repository maintainers for questions regarding ownership.
